## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the sovereign precision, timeless heritage, and architectural gravity of high-end Swiss horology. Crafted for collectors, connoisseurs, and private bespoke clients, the experience balances uncompromising digital minimalism with the sensory depth of physical master watchmaking.

The aesthetic fuses **Architectural Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Glassmorphism**. Layouts employ deliberate negative space, razor-sharp alignment, and cinematic proportions reminiscent of fine editorial publishing. Visual tension is achieved by pairing deep, light-absorbing obsidian foundations with luminescent champagne gold accents, delicate frosted crystal strata, and whisper-thin metallic perimeters. The interface avoids frivolous micro-interactions in favor of slow, deliberate, friction-free transitions that evoke the silent movement of a tourbillon caliber.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an uncompromising dark spectrum that mirrors the anti-reflective crystal, darkened titanium, and polished obsidian of haute horlogerie ateliers.

- **Obsidian Foundations (`#0B0C10`, `#12141A`)**: Serve as the canvas, absorbing visual noise and imparting dramatic depth. Elevated surfaces utilize charcoal undertones (`#181B22`) to establish visual separation without introducing distracting contrasts.
- **Champagne Gold Accents (`#D4AF37`, `#E5C158`)**: Reserved strictly for focal indicators, active states, key metrics, and prestigious micro-details. Used with surgical restraint to preserve rarity and value.
- **Warm Ivory & Pearl (`#FDFBF7`, `#F4F0E8`)**: Applied to high-contrast headlines and editorial pull quotes, offering a softer, warmer, and more organic legibility than stark clinical white.
- **Muted Platinum & Steel (`#9CA3AF`, `#E5E7EB`)**: Used for technical readouts, secondary copy, captions, and structural grid rules.
- **Border Overlays**: Fine borders consistently draw from `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)` on interactive layers and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` on secondary containers.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy relies on the dynamic between **Playfair Display** and **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Playfair Display** introduces haute editorial elegance, commanding large headings, grand numbers, model denominations, and complication names. It is set with relaxed proportions and generous leading to mirror bespoke print books.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers clarity for specifications, continuous prose, caliber dimensions, and transactional mechanisms. Body weights lean toward Light (`300`) and Regular (`400`) to sustain an airy, uncrowded presence.
- **Tracking & Micro-Typography**: All uppercase labels, metadata indicators, serial stamps, and badge identifiers leverage aggressive positive tracking (`0.14em` to `0.22em`) to evoke technical dial engravings and Swiss certification marks.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by an architectural 12-column fluid grid on desktop, scaling down to an 8-column layout on tablet and a 4-column structure on mobile devices.

- **Negative Space**: Generous whitespace is an intentional indicator of prestige. Modules must never feel constrained. Large product imagery and complication showcases occupy full-bleed or wide multi-column spans.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Pacing between discrete case studies and timepiece showcases utilizes wide section margins (`3rem` to `6rem`), creating a contemplative cadence.
- **Responsive Adaptation**:
  - **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: 12 columns, `2rem` gutters, outer margin `4rem` (or capped maximum container width of `1440px` centered).
  - **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `2.5rem` outer margin.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1.5rem` outer margin. Vertical stacking prioritizes macro photography before technical specifications.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed without heavy, artificial dropshadows. Depth is articulated through **frosted crystal planes**, **tonal obsidian shifts**, and **micro-gold illumination**:

1. **Atelier Base (Ground)**: Deep obsidian `#0B0C10`, non-reflective, flat base.
2. **Sapphire Substrates (Level 1)**: Translucent surface layers (`#12141A` at 85% opacity) backed by a hardware-accelerated blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`). Outlined with an ultra-thin hairline border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`).
3. **Floating Horological Docks & Overlays (Level 2)**: Modals, flyouts, and sticky navigation use `#181B22` at 70% opacity, paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(36px)` and an illuminated gold hairline perimeter (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.22)`).
4. **Luminescent Glow**: For focused or activated states, subtle champagne radial glows (`box-shadow: 0 0 35px -10px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`) recreate the appearance of low light glancing across polished precious metal.

## Shapes

The shape architecture is calibrated to `1` (Soft), balancing surgical precision with tactile grace:

- **Corner Radii**: Standard components employ `0.25rem` (4px) radii, echoing precision-cut sapphire crystals and brushed bevels. Card structures and elevated modal panels step up to `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- **Geometric Integrity**: Circular geometry is preserved strictly for dial representations, status chronometers, and astronomical moonphase complications.
- **Hairline Framing**: Contours favor crisp, razor-sharp boundaries over organic, rounded silhouettes. This deliberate restraint distinguishes the interface from mass-market consumer software and reinforces the precision of fine Swiss engineering.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Crown Gold)**: Champagne gold gradient fill (`#D4AF37` transitioning to `#C59F2E`), dark obsidian text (`#0B0C10`), uppercase `label-md` tracking, soft 4px corner radius. On hover, reveals a fine champagne outer glow.
- **Secondary Action (Platinum Ghost)**: Translucent dark background (`rgba(18, 20, 26, 0.6)`), 1px border in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`, warm ivory text (`#F4F0E8`). On hover, border opacity shifts to 100% gold and background subtly lightens.
- **Tertiary / Inline Link**: Warm ivory or champagne gold underline that expands outward from the center on hover, accompanied by an uppercase `label-md` label.

### Cards & Spec Sheets
- Constructed using frosted glassmorphic panels (`rgba(18, 20, 26, 0.75)`, 24px blur).
- Framed by a 1px border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`), softly elevated to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)` on cursor interaction.
- Card interiors are structured with deliberate hierarchy: large serif model headers, micro-tracked technical spec labels (`label-sm`), and muted platinum body details.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: Minimalist sunken trough (`#0E1015`) with a 1px baseline or border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Focus State: Border transitions seamlessly to champagne gold (`#D4AF37`), with a faint ambient gold aura. Text renders in `#FDFBF7`.
- Labels remain in uppercase `label-sm` in `#9CA3AF`, floating cleanly above the input area.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Architectural and low-profile. Dark translucent base with hairline platinum/gold border.
- Selected state turns the background into subtle gold dust (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`), with gold text and a solid 1px champagne border.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square (checkbox) with 2px corners, or perfectly circular (radio).
- Default: Obsidian fill with a 1px muted border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`).
- Selected: Champagne gold core mark with a crisp, fine golden border enclosure.

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Complication Metric Pill**: High-precision indicators for power reserve, water resistance, and caliber frequency, rendered in monospaced or high-tracked sans-serif alongside a gold status pip.
- **Curated Watch Showcase Strip**: Horizontal full-bleed snap carousel with micro-dash pagination rendered as slender gold balance bars.
